## Objectives

**By the end of this lesson, learners will be able to:**

### Knowledge
- Distinguish vague objectives from measurable ones in the 5 items of the end-of-lesson quiz, without notes, naming the invisible verb or the missing standard where there is one, with at least 4 of 5 items correct. [K1]

### Skills
- Rewrite 3 vague objectives from their own teaching on task sheet M01-L1-1, each with a verb that can be watched, conditions and a countable standard, with all 3 rewrites passing all 3 lines of the check on the sheet. [S1]

### Autonomy and responsibility
- Take the initiative to check the 3 rewrites against the 3-line check and correct every failing line before posting them on the Q&A board, within 2 days of watching the video. [A1]

## Competency element

This lesson builds the competency element "Tell whether an objective can be checked, and repair one that cannot". Link to the module objectives: it is the first half of the M01 skill objective "Write lesson objectives a colleague could assess without asking the author what was meant".

## What learners produce in this lesson

- One repaired objective on paper, written at the 90-second pause: practice for S1, not collected.
- Task sheet M01-L1-1 with 3 rewritten objectives and the check column filled, posted on the Q&A board: evidence for S1 and A1.

## Assumptions

- One-video mode: 7 minutes, target 980-1050 words, script 1005 words.
- The count leaves out stage directions in square brackets and the `[INSTRUCTOR STORY: ...]` placeholder. Filled with 1-2 sentences, the script stays inside the 10% margin.
- The program file is a micro-course in minutes and gives this lesson `video_minutes` 7, theory 7, practice 15, assessment 5. That makes it one video, not several.
- The project has an `assessments/` folder, so section 6 of the content file carries the objectives to measure, a blueprint and the pass mark, and no quiz items. The script says "5 questions, pass at 4"; the blueprint uses the same numbers.
- Pause time (30 + 15 + 90 + 15 + 30 seconds = 3 minutes) is counted as practice, on top of the 7 minutes of video.
- Script length is planned at 145 spoken words per minute.
- Made up for illustration, and said so in the script: Priya, her Year 6 class and the 9 of 27 pupils (lead-in and close), and the sales coach in 2.2. The example lines on slides 5 to 11 are written for this lesson.
- Placeholder the instructor fills before recording: one `[INSTRUCTOR STORY: ...]` in the lead-in (the first objective you wrote that nobody could check).
- Lesson 2 is taken to be "The four parts of an objective", as the program lists it.
- The 2-day deadline and the pass level "all 3 rewrites pass all 3 lines" are numbers chosen for this lesson.
